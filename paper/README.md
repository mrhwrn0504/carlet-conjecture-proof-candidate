# Papers in this repository

This repository keeps two mathematically related but logically distinct works.

## Lean formalization paper

`lean-formalization/main_en.tex` and `main_zh.tex` describe the proof route encoded in the included Lean development. The matching PDFs are built from those TeX sources. The Lean proof covers the odd-degree statement with the original quantifiers; it does not formalize the separate reciprocal-geometric candidate below.

## Revised proof-candidate manuscript

`original-proof-candidate/` contains Chinese and English PDF editions of Wang Quanzhi's August 2026 proof-candidate manuscript, TeX sources for the Chinese edition and English front matter/addendum, and the supplied finite-certificate verifier. The Chinese edition is re-typeset and includes clarifications. The English edition uses a revised title page and supplement with the supplied English proof-body pages.

The manuscript itself labels the general reciprocal-geometric proof as a candidate pending independent specialist review. Its three finite certificates pass the exact supplied verifier; that does not certify the general argument or amount to Lean formalization. No DOCX files are distributed. Both editions are detailed in the subdirectory README.
