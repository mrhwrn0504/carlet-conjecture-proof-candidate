## Target

Over (K=\mathbb F_{2^n}) with **odd** (n\ge 7), set
(Q=2^n), (q(t)=t^2+t), (D(t)=q(t)^3+q(t)+1).
We seek (t\in K) with (D(t)\ne0) and all three values
[
\operatorname{Tr}(1/D(t)),\quad
\operatorname{Tr}(q(t)^2/D(t)),\quad
\operatorname{Tr}(t^6/D(t))
]
equal to zero. For odd (n), this entails (q(t)\ne0)
(since \(\operatorname{Tr}(1)=1\)) and (q(t)+1\ne0)
(since (X^2+X+1) has no root in an odd extension).

## Reduction from seven to five character sums

For (a,b,c\in\mathbb F_2) write
[
S_{abc}=\sum_{D(t)\ne0}
(-1)^{\operatorname{Tr}((a+bq(t)^2+ct^6)/D(t))}.
]
The translation (t\mapsto t+1) preserves (q,D), and
[
(t+1)^6=t^6+q(t)^2+1.
]
Thus (S_{abc}=S_{a+c,b+c,c}). In particular,
(S_{001}=S_{111}) and (S_{101}=S_{011}).

Let (r=\#\{t:D(t)=0\}) and (N_n) count parameters satisfying
all three trace-zero tests. Character orthogonality gives the exact
**five-sum** formula
[
\boxed{8N_n=Q-r+S_{100}+S_{010}+S_{110}
             +2S_{001}+2S_{101}.}
]
Moreover (N_n) is even: all good parameters occur in the
two-element orbits ({t,t+1}).

## Six simple poles and genus five

Over (\mathbb F_2[t]),
[
D(t)=t^6+t^5+t^4+t^3+t^2+t+1
=(t^3+t+1)(t^3+t^2+1).
]
Both cubic factors are irreducible and distinct, giving six simple
geometric roots. For every nonzero ((a,b,c)), the numerator
(A_{abc}=a+b(t^4+t^2)+ct^6) has gcd 1 with (D).
The independent script checks all seven polynomial gcds;
one may check only five by translation symmetry.

The Artin--Schreier cover
(y^2+y=A_{abc}(t)/D(t)) has six simple poles and no
pole at infinity. Its smooth projective normalization is geometrically
connected and has genus
(g=(-2+6(1+1))/2=5).

For odd (n), the fibre at infinity has (e_\infty=2)
rational points if (c=0), and (e_\infty=0) if (c=1),
because the function takes the value (c) at infinity.
Each rational simple pole contributes one totally ramified point.
Consequently the curve point count is
[
\#C_{abc}(K)=Q+S_{abc}+e_\infty.
]

By the classical Hasse--Weil curve bound,
(\#C(K)\ge Q+1-10\sqrt Q), so
[
S_{abc}\ge\begin{cases}
-10\sqrt Q-1,&c=0,\\
-10\sqrt Q+1,&c=1.
\end{cases}
]
There are three nonzero triples with (c=0) and four with (c=1),
so the total contribution from all seven nontrivial characters is at
least (1-70\sqrt Q). We obtain the **improved** lower bound
[
\boxed{N_n\ge\frac{Q-r+1-70\sqrt Q}{8}
            \ge\frac{Q-70\sqrt Q-5}{8}.}
]
At (Q\ge2^{13}=8192), this is positive. Thus the exact V7
three-trace witness exists for every odd (n\ge13) at the level of
ordinary mathematics, assuming the classical genus and Hasse--Weil
theorems. The constant (-5) improves the older (-13) estimate.
The five-sum symmetry reduces the number of distinct character-sum
instances needed for an eventual Lean implementation.
